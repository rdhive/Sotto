## Sotto 0.2.0: a new look

Sotto has been redesigned from the ground up. The cassette-deck interface is
gone. In its place is a clean, Apple-style app that follows your Windows
light or dark setting. Dictation, the speech engine, your dictionary and
your history all work exactly as before.

![Sotto 0.2.0 main window](https://github.com/rdhive/Sotto/raw/main/docs/images/hero-light.png)

### What's new

**Recorder**
- One button to start and stop dictation. The red circle turns into a stop square while recording.
- A live scrolling waveform and a large timer. Your last take stays visible after you stop.

**Transcriptions and dictionary**
- Transcriptions are grouped by day, with dictionary fixes shown inline (`cloud code → Claude Code`).
- Copy and delete appear when you hover over an item.
- A cleaner dictionary editor that shows what each correction will also catch.

**Settings**
- Grouped into Shortcuts, Recognition and Application.
- Real on/off switches, and shortcuts shown as keycaps.
- A new settings button in the main window title bar.

**Everywhere**
- Follows Windows light and dark mode, including the title bar, and switches live.
- The floating recording indicator is now a compact dark pill with a mini waveform and timer.
- Ctrl + F jumps to search. Esc cancels a dictionary edit.
- Better contrast on buttons in dark mode.

### Install

1. Download **Sotto-Setup-0.2.0.exe** below and run it.
2. Already on 0.1.0? Quit Sotto from the tray icon (right-click → Exit) first. The installer upgrades in place and keeps your settings, dictionary and history.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), an NVIDIA GPU with CUDA 12 support. [Ollama](https://ollama.com) with `qwen2.5:3b-instruct` is optional, for smarter cleanup.

> [!NOTE]
> The installer is not code-signed yet. If SmartScreen shows *Windows protected your PC*, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
