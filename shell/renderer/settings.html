Settings

Shortcuts

Push to talk

Hold to dictate. Handled by the engine, so it works even when Sotto is not in the foreground.

Start and stop

Toggles dictation from any application.

Recognition

Speech model

Larger is more accurate and slower.

Language

Pinning one is faster and avoids misdetection on short clips.

Cleanup

Removes fillers and restores punctuation. Use a non-reasoning instruct model: reasoning models return their working out as the answer.

Microphone

Avoid anything marked as system audio. That is what your speakers play, not your voice.

Changing the speech model or microphone takes effect the next time the engine starts.

Application

Start with Windows

Launches Sotto at sign-in so dictation is ready without opening anything.

Recording indicator

Shows a small floating pill with level and time while you dictate into another app.

Speech engine

Started and stopped with the app. Nothing to run by hand.
