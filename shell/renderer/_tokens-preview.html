VU meter — live ballistics

Driven by a synthetic speech envelope here; the real one takes RMS from the engine. Ballistics are instrument constants, not taste: 12 ms attack, 300 ms decay, 900 ms peak hold. The needle is integrated per frame on canvas — nothing re-renders on audio callbacks.

Readout well — LED counter

88:88.8 00:02.6

Unlit segments stay visible behind the value, the way a real seven-segment display does — the ghost 88:88.8 is always there. Fine horizontal ruling across the face reads as the filter in front of the LEDs. The bloom is 5px and lives inside the recessed well; it never touches type outside one.

LED ladder — peak indicator

-20-10 -30+6

Runs off the same envelope as the needle. Green to −3, amber to 0, red above. The floating segment is peak hold — same ballistics constants as the needle.

Fasteners and vents sit at real structural positions — corners of a plate, above a heat source — never repeated as pattern. An engraved legend inverts its shadow against a printed one, which is what separates a metal plate from a plastic panel.
